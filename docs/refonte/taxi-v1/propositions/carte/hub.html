Questions

Is the transfer private?

Yes. Every transfer is private, with no sharing, so you travel only with your group.

How do I pay?

In cash to the driver on the day of the transfer. There is no prepayment and no hidden fees.

Can I travel with children or babies?

Yes. The request form lets you add children and babies and ask for a child seat or a baby seat.

Is it a taxi or a minivan?

Outside Koh Lanta we always drive a private minivan. On the island itself you can also book a taxi.

Do you only drive from Koh Lanta?

No. Koh Lanta (also written Ko Lanta) is our base, but we drive across Southern Thailand, including trips that never touch the island, such as Krabi Airport to Ao Nang or Phuket Airport to Khao Lak. Tell us where we pick you up and where you are going in the request form.

Can you take me to the airport when I leave Koh Lanta?

Yes. Enter your hotel or Koh Lanta as the pickup and the airport as the destination, with your date and pickup time. We drive to Krabi, Phuket and Surat Thani airports.

Can you drive me to a pier such as Donsak or Cheow Lan?

Yes. Donsak Pier and Cheow Lan Pier are among the places we drive to. Enter the pier as your pickup or destination in the request form.

Can I book day tours or boat trips too?

Day tours around Koh Lanta and boat trips are handled by Secret Travel & Tour. You can choose a trip and ask for a price on their page.
