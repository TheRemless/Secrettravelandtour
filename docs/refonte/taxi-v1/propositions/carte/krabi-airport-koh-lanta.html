Request this transfer

Give us your flight number and landing time, your hotel on Koh Lanta and the number of people. The team confirms the price on WhatsApp and the driver waits for you at the airport.

For the way back, enter your hotel as the pickup and Krabi Airport as the destination, with the time you want to leave.

Questions about this route

Where does the driver meet me at Krabi Airport?

At the time you give us, at the airport. Send your flight number and the team confirms the meeting point on WhatsApp.

Can I book the transfer back to Krabi Airport?

Yes. Enter your hotel as the pickup and Krabi Airport as the destination, with your date and pickup time.

Is the transfer private?

Yes. Every transfer is private, with no sharing, so you travel only with your group.

How do I pay?

In cash to the driver on the day of the transfer. There is no prepayment and no hidden fees.

Can I travel with children or babies?

Yes. The request form lets you add children and babies and ask for a child seat or a baby seat.
