Questions

How do I book a transfer?

Fill in the request form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and the team replies with the price and the details.

Is the transfer private?

Yes. Every transfer is private, with no sharing, so you travel only with your group.

Is it a taxi or a minivan?

Outside Koh Lanta we always drive a private minivan. On the island itself you can also book a taxi.

How do I pay?

You pay in cash to the driver on the day of the transfer. There is no prepayment and no hidden fees.

Where do you drive in Southern Thailand?

Across Southern Thailand: Krabi Town and Krabi airport, Ao Nang, Phuket and Phuket airport, Surat Thani airport and bus terminal, Trang, Khao Lak, Khao Sok, Donsak Pier and Cheow Lan Pier, and more.

Do you only drive from Koh Lanta?

No. Koh Lanta (also written Ko Lanta) is our base, but we drive across Southern Thailand, including trips that never touch the island, such as Krabi Airport to Ao Nang or Phuket Airport to Khao Lak. Tell us where we pick you up and where you are going in the request form.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. We drive private minivan transfers between Krabi, Phuket and Surat Thani airports and Koh Lanta, Ao Nang, Khao Lak or any other place in Southern Thailand, in both directions.

How do I get from Krabi Airport to Koh Lanta?

Book a private minivan from Krabi Airport (KBV) to Koh Lanta. Enter Krabi Airport as the pickup and Koh Lanta as the destination in the request form, and the team replies with the price and the details.

How do I get from Phuket Airport to Khao Lak?

Book a private minivan from Phuket Airport (HKT) to Khao Lak. Enter Phuket Airport as the pickup and Khao Lak as the destination in the request form, and the team replies with the price.

Can you pick me up at Phuket Airport and take me to Koh Lanta?

Yes. We drive private minivan transfers from Phuket Airport (HKT) to Koh Lanta and back. Send your date and time in the request form and the team replies with the price.

Can you take me to the airport when I leave Koh Lanta?

Yes. Enter your hotel or Koh Lanta as the pickup and the airport as the destination, with your date and pickup time. We drive to Krabi, Phuket and Surat Thani airports.

Can you drive me to a pier such as Donsak or Cheow Lan?

Yes. Donsak Pier and Cheow Lan Pier are among the places we drive to. Enter the pier as your pickup or destination in the request form.

Can I travel with children or babies?

Yes. The request form lets you add children and babies and ask for a child seat or a baby seat.

Can I book day tours or boat trips too?

Day tours around Koh Lanta and boat trips are handled by Secret Travel & Tour. You can choose a trip and ask for a price on their page.
